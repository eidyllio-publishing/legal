Privacy Notice

Atlas Global Ventures · last updated 31 July 2026

This notice describes how Atlas Global Ventures handles information in connection with its internal advertising-management application, which connects to the Amazon Ads API.

Who we are

Atlas Global Ventures is an independent publishing company. Its imprints publish books sold through retail platforms including Amazon.

What this application is

The application is used solely internally, by Atlas Global Ventures, to manage and report on its own advertising campaigns for its own published titles. It is not offered to the public, has no external users, no sign-up, and is not a product or service made available to any third party.

What information it accesses

Through the Amazon Ads API, the application accesses advertising data belonging to Atlas Global Ventures' own advertising account — campaign settings, keywords, budgets, and aggregate performance metrics such as impressions, clicks, spend, and attributed sales.

It does not access, collect, request, receive, or store personal information about customers, readers, or any other individuals. The metrics it reads are aggregate campaign statistics, not records about identifiable people.

How the information is used

Exclusively to operate and improve Atlas Global Ventures' own advertising: reviewing campaign performance, adjusting budgets and keywords, and deciding where to invest advertising spend. The data is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

The information is not sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party. It is not used for advertising to individuals, profiling, or automated decision-making about any person.

Storage and security

Data retrieved from the Amazon Ads API is stored on systems controlled by Atlas Global Ventures and is accessible only to the company. API credentials are held in encrypted operating-system credential storage and are never committed to source control or written to logs.

Retention

Advertising performance data is retained only as long as it is useful for operating the company's own campaigns and meeting its accounting and tax obligations, after which it is deleted.

Your rights

Because this application does not collect personal information about individuals, there is ordinarily no personal data for an individual to access, correct, or erase. If you believe Atlas Global Ventures holds information about you and wish to enquire, contact us using the address below and we will respond.
